THE INTERMEDIATE WIP BLACK HOLE

Why the Supermarket System Was Built

Between cutting table output and sewing line feeding lies the "supermarket zone"—a massive temporary holding area with hundreds of racks storing cut garment bundles. Without digital allocation, cut parts were frequently misplaced, mixed up, or aged past delivery deadlines.

Pre-Supermarket Problems

  • Lost Cut Bundles: Cut components for sleeve, body, and collar sat on random racks with no coordinate mapping, taking hours to locate.
  • Non-FIFO Feeding: Older cut lots were buried under freshly cut bundles, leading to fabric shade variations and delivery delays.
  • Unplanned Rack Congestion: Supermarket racks overflowed without capacity visibility, causing cut panels to get crumpled on the floor.
  • Discrepancies Between Cut vs Input: No digital audit trail tracked the exact moment cut pieces were handed over to sewing line feeders.

The Digital Supermarket Solution

  • Physical Rack & Bin Allocation: Every cut bundle is digitally scanned and assigned to a precise Zone, Rack, and Shelf position.
  • FIFO Dispatch Workflow: System prompts line feeders to pick the oldest cut lot first, enforcing strict First-In, First-Out integrity.
  • Real-Time Rack Occupancy Map: Visual heatmaps indicating rack utilization, free shelf slots, and over-capacity warnings.
  • Stock Adjustment Audit Logs: Cryptographically logged inventory corrections when bundle quantities are modified or scrapped.

Executive Dashboard

Real-time shopfloor wallboard displaying Total Active Bins, Occupied Bins Ratio, Total Bundles in Supermarket, and Total Pieces staged across physical racks.

02

Rack Allocation Terminal

Cut bundle receiving interface. Scans bundle QR/barcode, validates against PO master, and assigns physical Rack ID and shelf slot with instant capacity check.

03

Sewing Line Dispatch

Enforces FIFO bundle issuance. Verifies target sewing line, deducts supermarket WIP, records dispatch timestamp, and notifies line chiefs.

04

Manage Racks & Topology

Dynamic warehouse layout editor configuring rack counts (Racks 1 to 33), shelf levels, maximum piece thresholds, and maintenance status.

05

Search & Location Lookup

Instant locator search across PO Number, Style, Color, or Bundle Tag, returning exact physical rack coordinates in milliseconds.

06

Stock Adjustment & Scrap Log

Strict reconciliation interface for re-sorting or damaged parts, tracking operator ID, reason code, and adjusting live inventory counts.

Key Takeaway: My Inventory Engineering Impact

I developed the Cutting Supermarket System to resolve the intermediate WIP black hole between our cutting floor and sewing lines. I engineered the physical-to-digital coordinate mapping for 33 physical industrial racks and hundreds of bin locations, built barcode-driven bundle allocation and FIFO picking workflows, and implemented an ACID-compliant transaction backend. My system drastically cut bundle search and retrieval time, reduced floor worker workload, eradicated misplaced garment bundles, and gave factory planners real-time visibility into intermediate buffer stocks.
