MANUAL REPORTING FRICTION

Why Cutting Report Was Built

Previously, compiling cutting floor daily progress required supervisors to manually collect tally papers from 12+ spreading and cutting tables, spend 2 hours typing them into Excel, and email delayed summaries that arrived after night shift planning was already locked.

Pre-Automation Inefficiencies

  • 2+ Hours Wasted Daily: Supervisors spent high-value management time doing manual data entry instead of optimizing fabric spreading.
  • Human Transcription Errors: Typos in Excel formulas caused incorrect order balances and duplicate cuts.
  • Blind Bundle Status: Merchandisers calling to check if a specific PO had finished cutting had to wait hours for manual physical verification.
  • No Live Variance Tracking: Planned cut quantities were frequently under-cut or over-cut without immediate alerts.

The Automated Solution

  • Instant GPRO MES Data Harvesting: Queries cutting transaction tables in real time with zero manual entry.
  • Comprehensive Bundle Enquiry: Search any CutLot, PO, or bundle number to see its exact status, ply count, and cutter operator.
  • Multi-Dimensional Slicing: Filter instantly across Date Range, Brand/Buyer, Style Number, MO, Color, and Target Sewing Line.
  • Export-Ready Reporting: One-click export to clean Excel sheets formatted for executive production meetings.
