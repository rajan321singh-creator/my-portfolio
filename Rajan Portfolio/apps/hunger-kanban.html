CRITICAL SHOPFLOOR BOTTLENECK

The Cost of "Starving" Sewing Lines

In apparel manufacturing, sewing operators represent the highest direct labor cost. When the cutting floor cuts the wrong orders or delays feeding, sewing lines run dry ("starvation"), causing massive productivity drops and expensive delivery delays.

Pre-Kanban Reality

  • Blind Cutting Schedules: Cutting supervisors cut orders based on paper plans rather than what sewing lines were actually consuming in real time.
  • Idle Sewing Operators: Entire lines of 40–60 operators sat idle waiting for cut bundles, burning hundreds of non-productive hours.
  • Lagging Report Data: Traditional WIP reports were generated in Excel at the end of the day—hours too late to prevent line shutdowns.
  • Supermarket Inventory Hiding: Cut pieces sat forgotten in intermediate storage racks while line supervisors scrambled for work.

The Hunger Kanban Solution

  • Real-Time MES Telemetry: Live stream querying GPRO MES directly every 120 seconds to compute exact piece counts inside each line.
  • Color-Coded Priority Alerts: Instant visual cues (Red: Critical WIP Gap, Orange: Hungry, Green: Balanced) forcing proactive replenishment.
  • Cutting Priority Filtering: Automatically hides healthy lines and surfaces only POs that the cutting room needs to cut right now.
  • Supermarket WIP Visibility: Shows cut pieces ready in Supermarket waiting to enter Sewing Input, bridging the departmental gap.
THREE SPECIALIZED PRODUCTION TABS

Production Tabs & Features

Tailored interface modes engineered for the specific daily needs of Cutting managers, Sewing line chiefs, and Industrial Engineering leadership.

TAB 1

Cutting Floor Priority View

Dedicated queue for cutting supervisors. Filters out healthy lines and ranks hungry lines by severity, showing exactly which POs and colorways must be laid and cut immediately.

TAB 2

Sewing Line WIP Drilldown

Provides sewing line chiefs an instant breakdown of active orders, current line WIP (Sewing Input minus Sewing Output), Supermarket In stock ready for feeding, and balance remaining to input.

TAB 3

Executive Summary Wallboard

Plant-wide overview designed for large overhead TV displays and operations leadership to monitor factory-wide WIP balance and pacing across all manufacturing units.

FEATURE

Hands-Free TV Auto-Scroll

Automated viewport cycling designed for shopfloor wallboards. Automatically scrolls through active production lines every 15 seconds without requiring manual interaction.

FEATURE

GPRO MES Direct Bridge

Pulls transactional station records directly from SQL Server with zero manual re-entry, calculating true line WIP dynamically from station barcode scans.

FEATURE

Supermarket Buffer Integration

Instantly reveals cut bundles sitting in intermediate supermarket racks, preventing redundant recutting when work is already prepared.

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION

High-Throughput Polling Engine

Engineered with a lightweight Node.js Express server that aggregates heavy SQL queries in memory, protecting the operational MES database while serving dozens of real-time clients.

Key Takeaway: My Operational Impact

I built Hunger Kanban to eliminate the costliest bottleneck on our apparel shopfloor: idle sewing lines waiting for cut fabric. Instead of relying on delayed daily reports, I tapped directly into the GPRO MES SQL database, architecting a real-time polling pipeline that computes line deficit levels dynamically. I engineered three specialized views for Cutting supervisors, Line chiefs, and Industrial Engineering managers. My system eradicated sewing line starvation, gave cutting rooms proactive feeding buffers, reduced supervisor workload, and significantly boosted overall factory throughput.
