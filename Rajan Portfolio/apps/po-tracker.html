DISCONNECTED ENTERPRISE SYSTEMS

Why Unified PO Tracker Was Built

Apparel manufacturing runs on two separate technological worlds: the enterprise ERP (which manages buyer orders, bills of materials, and shipping dates) and the factory MES (which tracks shopfloor machine operations and sewing line bundles). Because these systems did not communicate, nobody knew where a buyer's order actually was.

The Information Chasm

  • Siloed ERP vs MES: ERP showed an order was "In Production" for 3 weeks, with zero visibility into whether pieces were at cutting, sewing, or finishing.
  • Endless Phone Calls: Merchandisers spent hours daily calling cutting supervisors, line chiefs, and finishing managers to locate specific POs.
  • The Metal Detection Blindspot: Garments finished sewing but sat uncounted at Metal Detection (MD) before warehouse receipt.
  • Missed Export Vessels: Unnoticed bottlenecks in packing caused containers to miss port cutoff times, incurring massive airfreight costs.

The Unified PO Solution

  • End-to-End Traceability: Tracks every PO through 6 distinct operations: Cutting (55), Input (75), Output (250), MD, Pack, Ship.
  • Split-Screen Verification: Direct side-by-side comparison between GPRO MES shopfloor scans and STAGE ERP warehouse records.
  • Interactive KPI Dashboard: Real-time high-level aggregate counters across active orders in the last 90 days.
  • Natural Language Assistant: Integrated "Ask Assistant" widget enabling users to query order bottlenecks in plain English.

Key Takeaway: My Systems Integration Impact

I architected the Unified PO Tracker to bridge the critical information chasm between our enterprise ERP and shopfloor GPRO MES. Previously, locating an order required dozens of daily phone calls across cutting, sewing, and packing floors. I developed high-performance reconciliation middleware that ingests transactional scans across 6 distinct manufacturing stages (Cutting, Sewing Input, Sewing Output, Metal Detection, Packing, and Final Shipping). I also integrated an AI-assisted query dialog and real-time KPI scorecards. My system drastically cut status inquiry calls, reduced planner and supervisor workload, and gave factory leadership complete, real-time order visibility across all manufacturing stages.
