PRE-SEWING DEFECT PREVENTION

Why CPI Was Built

If a defective cut fabric piece (with shade variation, fabric slub, hole, or knife notch flaw) enters the sewing line, it takes 30 minutes of sewing labor before being rejected at final garment inspection—costing 10x more to fix than catching it at the cutting table.

Cutting Floor Inspection Gaps

  • Paper Tally Sheets: Cut piece defect audits were handwritten on paper checklists with zero aggregation or instant escalation.
  • Delayed Replacement Panels: When a defective panel was found, getting a replacement cut from leftover fabric rolls took hours of manual paperwork.
  • No Defect Pareto by Fabric Roll: Quality managers could not trace which fabric roll or cutting spreader caused recurring slubs or shade shading.
  • Unverified Bundle Integrity: Bundles entered sewing with missing pieces, causing operator stoppages mid-line.

The CPI Workstation Solution

  • Instant QR & Barcode Scanning: Works with optical laser scanners or webcam QR readers via embedded jsQR engine with instant audio tone cues.
  • Touch Defect Categorization: Inspectors tap defect types (Shading, Hole, Cut Flaw, Yarn Defect, Notch Error) in 2 clicks.
  • Automated Replacement Requisition: Generates digital replacement panel tickets directly to the re-cut table.
  • Bidirectional MES Sync: Updates GPRO MES cut lot bundle status (Inspected, Pass, Hold, Rework) in real time.

Numbering Card & Dynamic QR Generation

The core digital backbone of the cutting floor. Transforms raw manual lay sheets into intelligent, serialized cut bundle numbering records. Automatically computes exact ply ranges, ratio splits, and shade lots, generating high-density 2D QR codes on thermal bundle cards ready for high-speed scanning.

Thermal Bundle Card Printing

Direct driver-free printing to industrial Zebra and TSC thermal tag printers. Emits standardized cut bundle cards with scannable QR codes, buyer PO specifications, garment sizes, and cutter operator IDs.

03

CPI Inspection Workstation

High-speed touch workstation. Features instant barcode scanning, Web Audio pass/fail tones, component verification, and 1-tap defect logging.

04

Reports & PO Analytics

Historical quality logs, defect rate percentages by PO, roll-level defect clustering, and rejection slip export to Excel.

05

Dashboard & Hourly Metrics

Visual wallboard tracking inspector pace, hourly panels checked, pass rates, and Pareto charts of top defects.

06

Admin & Master Configuration

Define factory tables, defect taxonomies (Hole, Shade, Notch, Slub), user roles, and tolerance settings.

Key Takeaway: My Quality Engineering Impact

I engineered the Cut Panel Inspection (CPI) terminal to eliminate defective fabric pieces before they consume expensive sewing line labor. I designed the touch-first interface specifically for shopfloor tablet conditions, integrating browser-native Web Audio tone synthesizer for eyes-free pass/fail feedback and embedded camera QR scanning. By catching defects at the cut-piece stage rather than final inspection, my solution drastically accelerated re-cut turnaround times, reduced technician workload, and prevented defective panels from contaminating active sewing lines.
