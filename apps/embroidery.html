EMBELLISHMENT BOTTLENECK

Why the Embroidery Module Was Built

Embroidery is one of the most error-prone secondary operations in garment manufacturing. Traditional plants tracked multi-head Tajima and Barudan embroidery machines using paper logbooks, leading to lost bundles, wrong thread tension defect batches, and untracked machine downtime.

Paper-Based Disadvantages

  • Piles of Dirty Paper Slips: Operators recorded piece counts and thread breaks on oil-stained paper slips that frequently went missing.
  • Wrong Machine Allocation: Bundles were loaded onto the wrong embroidery machine without automated cut lot parameter checks.
  • Late Defect Detection: Quality defects (birdnesting, thread breaks, skipped stitches) were discovered only after entire bundles were completed.
  • No Machine Utilization Data: Management had no way of knowing which embroidery heads were running or sitting idle.

The Digital Touchscreen Solution

  • One-Tap Machine Selection: Visual touch grid representing every physical embroidery machine on the floor for instant assignment.
  • Cut Lot Size Grouping: Automatically groups cut lots by size and color ratio to optimize multi-head embroidery runs.
  • Instant QC Logging: Dedicated QA inspection view to approve bundles (PASS) or log re-stitch / scrap with defect codes.
  • Automated GPRO MES Sync: Seamlessly clears bundle embroidery operations in the master MES database.

Embroidery Execution Suite (5 Primary Tabs)

01

Machine-Wise Production Capture

Operator touchscreen terminal. Select specific embroidery machine (Tajima / Barudan), scan bundle QR/barcode, and record live machine piece output.

02

Hourly Machine Pacing Matrix

Hour-by-hour output scorecard tracking pieces stitched machine by machine, pacing against shift targets, and logging operator changeovers.

03

Machine-Wise Production Analytics

Plant executive visualizer: machine-by-machine daily production output, active head utilization, operator shift tallies, and defect Pareto.

04

Reports & Piece-Rate Analysis

Historical shift production logs, operator piece-rate payroll summaries, and 1-click export to formatted Excel workbooks.

05

Master Settings & Defect Catalog

Manage machine inventory, active head counts (e.g. 12-head, 20-head), stitch count limits, and custom defect taxonomies.

06

Cut Lot Size Grouping Engine

Smart batching logic that groups matching bundle sizes together to minimize embroidery frame re-clamping and hooping adjustments.

Key Takeaway: My Operational Engineering Impact

I developed the Embroidery Module to digitize what was previously a completely opaque, paper-bound embellishment floor. I built high-contrast, glove-friendly touch terminals that allowed machine operators to assign bundles to multi-head Tajima and Barudan embroidery machines in a single tap. I integrated immediate quality defect logging and automated status synchronizations into our central MES database. My system eliminated paper slips, reduced operator workload, prevented misallocated embroidery batches, and significantly increased overall machine utilization across the plant.
