COMPLIANCE & VERIFICATION RISKS

Why Carton Scan RFID Was Built

Global athletic apparel brands mandate strict, flawless RFID EPC carton accuracy. If an export shipping container arrives at a brand distribution center with missing, unread, or mismatched RFID tags inside sealed master cartons, manufacturers face severe vendor chargebacks and shipment holds.

Pre-RFID Verification Risks

  • Vendor Chargeback Penalties: A single unscanned garment carton caused chargebacks and expensive penalties per shipment.
  • Midnight Shift Reconciliation Chaos: Factory night shifts crossing 00:00:00 caused standard software to split carton counts across two separate calendar days.
  • Unnoticed Defective Tags: Dead or de-tuned RFID inlays inside sealed boxes were not caught until the buyer opened the container overseas.
  • Manual Packing Slip Checks: Tallying high-volume cartons using paper clipboards was error-prone, labor-intensive, and sluggish.

The Automated RFID Solution

  • Real-Time Atma Cloud Integration: Direct connection to Avery Dennison Atma API captures table-top scanner reads with zero human delay.
  • Operational Shift Normalizer: Intelligently maps late-night scans (00:00 to 06:59) to the actual factory production shift date.
  • Instant Discrepancy Alerts: Flashes immediate alerts if a carton has duplicate EPCs, missing garments, or wrong size ratios.
  • End-to-End Verification: Delivers complete, audited shipping manifests for customs and brand quality assurance teams.
PLATFORM CAPABILITIES

Carton Verification Capabilities

01

Avery Dennison Atma API Polling

Seamless REST integration polling Avery Dennison RFID cloud reader controllers, streaming raw EPC carton scan telemetry directly into the client.

02

PO Carton Reconciliation

Validates total scanned cartons against master PO pack lists, tracking scanned vs expected pieces per size before cartons leave the facility.

03

Operational Shift Normalization

Automatically segments high-speed scans into Day Shift (07:00–19:00) vs Night Shift (19:00–07:00) while rolling 00:00–07:00 scans back to the active production date.

04

Discrepancy & EPC Diagnostics

Highlights duplicate EPC tags, flags incomplete cartons, and detects unread inlays in real time with visual amber and rose warnings.

05

Multi-Parametric Filter Toolbar

Instant client-side React filtering across PO Number, Buyer Code, Date Types (Created vs Updated), Shift Selection, and Completion Status.

06

Shipping Manifest Exporter

One-click CSV/Excel export of verified RFID carton manifests ready for container stuffing inspections, buyer EDI portals, and customs documentation.

Key Takeaway: My IoT Engineering Impact

I engineered the Avery RFID Scan system to automate shipping verification and eliminate the risk of missing or mismatched RFID tags in export garments. By integrating directly with Avery Dennison's Atma cloud API and developing custom cross-midnight shift reconciliation logic, I gave factory supervisors and shipping teams real-time visibility over every carton scanned on the Avery Dennison table-top RFID workstation, ensuring complete dispatch accuracy, eliminating manual counting workload, and preventing expensive customer chargebacks.
