WORKPLACE HEALTHCARE GAPS

Why Medical Management Was Built

In manufacturing plants employing large-scale factory workforces, on-site clinics treat dozens of employees daily for minor injuries, fevers, and chronic ailments. Paper prescription registers led to unverified sick leaves, expired pharmaceutical stock sitting on shelves, and zero morbidity data for factory leadership.

Paper Clinic Pitfalls

  • Expired Medicines on Shelves: Clinic stock had no automated expiry tracking, risking patient safety and regulatory audit fines.
  • Unverified Sick Leave Fraud: Paper sick slips were easily forged, causing unpredicted worker absenteeism across sewing lines.
  • Lost Patient History: Doctors had no record of an employee's recurring allergies, previous treatments, or chronic conditions.
  • Untracked Healthcare Costs: Hospital referral bills, lab charges, and ambulance expenses were buried in un-itemized receipts.

The Digital Medical Suite Solution

  • Digital OPD Visit Logging: Instant lookup by Employee ID, logging vitals (BP, Pulse, Temp), diagnosis, and authorized rest hours.
  • Automated Batch Expiry Tracking: Strict First-Expiry First-Out (FEFO) dispensing with automated alerts well in advance of drug expiry.
  • Chronic Illness Registry: Dedicated profiles for employees with hypertension, diabetes, or asthma with mandatory checkup schedules.
  • Medical Expense Management: Itemized tracking of outside hospital bills, referral treatments, and emergency transports.

Drug Expiry & Safety Alerts

Visual amber/red badges for pharmaceutical batches approaching expiration, preventing compliance violations during health ministry audits.

04

Chronic Care & Health Monitoring

Active registry for vulnerable workers (hypertension, diabetes, asthma) with automated recurring checkup reminders and medication schedules.

05

Medical Expenses & Referrals

Logs outside hospitalizations, emergency ambulance runs, external specialist consultations, and diagnostic lab fees with receipt uploads.

06

Morbidity Analytics & OSHA Reports

Aggregates illness trends across seasons (e.g. seasonal flu spikes, heat stress), allowing factory HR to take proactive preventive measures.
