WAREHOUSE BOTTLENECK & AUDIT RISKS

Why WH Stock & Rack Was Built

Finished Goods warehouses store tens of thousands of export cartons across multiple aisles. When cartons were placed haphazardly without structured PO-to-rack planning, warehouse staff spent hours searching for orders. Crucially, when cartons were opened for QA or buyer audits, lack of systematic Re-Metal Detection (Re-MD) tracking created serious needle contamination and audit compliance risks.

Warehouse & Audit Vulnerabilities

  • Massive Disorganized Spreadsheets: Managing 140MB+ CSV spreadsheets crashed standard computers and caused stale inventory data.
  • Blind Carton Slotting: Cartons were placed in random bins without advance PO rack planning, causing chaotic export staging.
  • Untracked Post-Audit Cartons: Cartons opened for QA inspection were prone to needle contamination if not strictly re-scanned through Re-MD.
  • Untracked Aging Stock: Delayed customer POs sat on premium warehouse rack space for months unnoticed.

The Digital Warehouse Solution

  • High-Performance Data Engine: Custom Python backend indexing over 140MB of inventory logs with sub-second querying.
  • PO Rack Planning: Pre-assigns incoming PO quantities to designated rack bays, aisles, and shelf tiers for structured slotting.
  • Re-MD Carton Tracking: Automatically tracks cartons pulled for QA/buyer audits through Re-Metal Detection before restaging.
  • Automated Stock Aging Alerts: Highlights cartons dwelling on racks for prompt dispatch escalation.

My platform drastically reduced forklift staging workload, eliminated misplaced orders, and guaranteed 100% metal-free audit compliance before dispatch.
