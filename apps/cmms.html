THE SHOPFLOOR BOTTLENECK

Why CMMS Was Built

In high-volume garment manufacturing facilities with over 1,500+ sewing, cutting, and finishing machines, traditional maintenance was managed through paper work orders and manual whiteboards.

Operational Pain Points

  • Unplanned Line Stoppages: Lack of preventive maintenance tracking led to sudden sewing machine motor burnouts and needle feed failures during peak orders.
  • Untracked Machine Movements: Machinery moved across lines without digital logs, causing lost assets and phantom inventories.
  • Critical Spare Stockouts: Mechanics took spare parts without real-time inventory deductions, leaving the storeroom dry during critical breakdowns.
  • No MTTR / MTBF Telemetry: Management had zero visibility into mechanic response times or repeat machine failure rates.

The CMMS Solution

  • Automated PM Schedules: Calendar-driven preventive maintenance alerts ensuring every single machine is serviced before failure.
  • Machine Transfer Workflow: Rigorous digital sign-off when machines move between sewing lines or across factories.
  • Live Spare Issuance & Reorder Points: Automated minimum stock triggers preventing stockouts of needles, loopers, and motors.
  • Breakdown Telemetry & KPI Wallboard: Instant calculation of Mean Time To Repair (MTTR) and Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF).
ENGINEERING CAPABILITIES

Core Enterprise Modules & Production Tabs

01

Machine Master & Asset Registry

Comprehensive digital passport for every asset: model, serial number, motor specs, purchase date, current line assignment, and lifetime service history.

02

PM Execution & Management

Automated preventive maintenance frequency engine (weekly, monthly, quarterly) with standard servicing checklists and digital mechanic sign-offs.

03

Breakdown Management

Instant breakdown logging from sewing lines. Dispatches work orders to on-duty mechanics, records breakdown root cause, and timestamps resolution time.

04

Spare Parts & Issuance

Complete storeroom catalog with bin locations, minimum threshold safety stock alerts, and automated issuance linking parts consumed to specific machines.

05

Machine Relocation Transfers

Inter-line and inter-plant movement logging with digital custodian transfer approval, ensuring complete, real-time floor asset visibility.

06

Reports, MTTR & MTBF Analytics

Executive analytics calculating Mean Time To Repair, Mean Time Between Failures, technician response rates, and spare parts expenditure trends.

Key Takeaway: My Engineering & Operational Impact

I engineered CMMS to solve high-friction physical bottlenecks across our factory maintenance floor. I architected the relational database schema, established strict foreign-key integrity between machine assets and spare parts storerooms, deployed the production environment behind Nginx with custom SSL certificates, and developed intuitive mechanic workflows. By converting paper-based reactive repairs into schedule-driven preventive maintenance, my system significantly reduced technician workload, slashed breakdown downtime, and completely eliminated critical spare part stockouts.
