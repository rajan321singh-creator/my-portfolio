FINANCIAL DISBURSEMENT BOTTLENECK

Why Cheque Print Was Built

Apparel manufacturing corporations disburse millions of dollars monthly across hundreds of vendor cheques. Every commercial bank (Arab Bank, Jordan Kuwait Bank, Housing Bank, Bank of Jordan) utilizes a different physical cheque size, date format, and payee coordinate layout. Handwritten cheques led to bank rejections, clerical errors, and severe audit vulnerability.

Pre-Automation Financial Risks

  • Bank Clearing Rejections: Minor misalignments or spelling differences between numerical and written amounts resulted in expensive clearing return fees.
  • Bilingual Handwriting Errors: Converting large numbers into formal Arabic words (Tafqeet) manually caused frequent clerical mistakes.
  • Rigid Printer Templates: Traditional software could only print one specific bank layout; switching banks required calling expensive software vendors.
  • Lack of Payment Voucher Traceability: Cheques were issued without digital links to invoice numbers, purchase orders, or payment approvals.

The Precision Cheque Print Solution

  • Millimeter Coordinate Calibration: Interactive visual drag-and-drop editor allowing users to calibrate Payee, Date, Amount, and Cross-Stamp coordinates for any bank.
  • Bilingual Amount-in-Words (Tafqeet): Automated engine converting numbers into perfect legal English and formal Arabic currency text.
  • Payment Voucher Generator: Prints formal two-part payment vouchers (bank cheque + detachable signed expense voucher) on standard continuous or laser paper.
  • Cryptographic Audit Ledger: Every cheque issued, voided, or reprinted is permanently recorded in an immutable financial audit trail.

Strict voiding protocol requiring supervisor authorization and mandatory cancellation reason codes for damaged or voided cheques.
