SHOPFLOOR OUTPUT VISIBILITY

Why the Daily Production Report Was Built

In fast-paced garment plants, managing 30+ sewing lines requires continuous, trustworthy visibility into actual daily piece counts. Traditional manual counts and end-of-shift paper tallies created delayed feedback and inter-departmental friction.

Traditional Reporting Pitfalls

  • Disputed Output Numbers: Planning claimed 25,000 pieces were produced; Production claimed 22,000; the packing floor reported 20,500.
  • End-of-Day Blindness: Hourly production slowdowns were not noticed until shift end, when operational adjustments were impossible.
  • Manual Excel Consolidation: MIS clerks spent hours every evening reconciling multiple disparate departmental paper sheets.
  • Delayed Daily Decision Making: Plant leadership lacked an instant, single-screen dashboard showing exact daily pieces stitched per line.

The Automated Daily Production Solution

  • Direct MES Sensor Telemetry: Automatic data extraction from sewing line RFID end-of-line terminals (Sewing Output).
  • Line-by-Line Production: Displays real-time completed pieces, feeding counts, and operator pace across every active line.
  • Live Hourly Pacing: Tracks actual pieces produced per hour, highlighting slumping lines mid-shift for immediate intervention.
  • Single Source of Truth: Completely eliminated inter-departmental data arguments across 30+ sewing lines.
ANALYTIC CAPABILITIES & MODES

Daily Production Views & Analytical Modes

MODE 1

Planning View

Macro view for Planning & Merchandising teams. Evaluates order commitment fulfillment, plan target variance, and cumulative balance remaining to ship.

MODE 2

Production Floor View

Micro view for Line Chiefs & Industrial Engineers. Details sewing input, line output, active line WIP, and operator efficiency levels.

03

Hourly Production Pacing

Dynamic hour-by-hour output breakdown (Hour 1 to Hour 10), highlighting slumping lines early in the shift before daily quotas are missed.

04

Live Line WIP Balance Gap

Automatically calculates true Line WIP (Input - Output) to detect choking bottlenecks inside sewing lines before starvation hits end-of-line.

05

One-Click Daily MIS Exporter

Generates pre-formatted daily executive production sheets ready for morning management briefings with zero manual copy-paste.

06

Filter by Unit, Line, and Style

Dynamic multi-tier dropdowns allowing plant general managers to filter across multiple manufacturing units (Unit 1, Unit 2) instantaneously.

Key Takeaway: My Plant Operations Impact

I developed the Daily Production Report to eliminate the persistent reconciliation friction between our factory Planning and Production departments. By tapping directly into live MES station scans (Sewing Input and Sewing Output inspection), I created a single real-time source of truth. I engineered dual analytical modes allowing planners to assess target fulfillment while floor managers evaluate hourly line pace and operator throughput. My system eliminated tedious daily manual consolidation, significantly reduced planner workload, and permanently resolved inter-departmental data disputes across sewing lines.
